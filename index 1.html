Our approach

Measured by capability, not fleet size.

Most maturity checklists ask whether a tool exists. We ask how the work actually gets done, from crew reassignment to disruption alerts, so a regional carrier running one aircraft type can score as high as a global network airline.

Every answer maps to a stage from 1 to 5. Your results show where you're strong, which dimension is holding the rest back, and what to do about it.

Five dimensions

What we assess.

Each dimension has four questions. Together they cover the airline from the data underneath to the people who have to adopt the change.

The model

Five stages of maturity.

Every answer option sits on one of these stages. The step from 3 to 4 is always about capability: information moves on its own, problems get flagged, and results get measured.
